Answer

Is solar plus battery worth it for Karimunjawa? The island runs on diesel.

Yes. Solar plus battery cuts the cost of electricity on Karimunjawa from 0.354 to 0.251 USD/kWh (-29.1%).

  • Near tie: 8 designs are within 1.0% of the least cost, from 6,750 to 8,250 kWp of PV.
  • Reserve case: if 1 diesel unit(s) must run in every hour, the least cost design is 1,500 kWp with 1,000 kWh at 0.336 USD/kWh, and the main design would cost 0.414 USD/kWh.
  • Diesel unit size: with units of 2,700 kW instead, diesel only costs 0.410 USD/kWh and the least cost design is 7,500 kWp with 19,000 kWh at 0.260 USD/kWh (36.7% below diesel only); if 1 diesel unit(s) must run in every hour, that design would cost 0.519 USD/kWh and the least cost design is 750 kWp with 0 kWh at 0.400 USD/kWh.
  • Island prices: PV and battery capital prices can rise 189.4% (PV to 3,239 USD/kWp, battery to 868 USD/kWh) before diesel only is cheaper.
  • Diesel engine hours: the least cost design cuts engine hours by 83.4% (1,458 against 8,760 unit hours a year), so overhaul and engine replacement costs, left out here, would favour it.
  • Land: the main design needs 8.3 ha of the 9.0 ha set aside (fits: yes).
  • Published design size: 1,244 kWp of PV with no battery gives 0.336 USD/kWh.
  • Cost: this study used 0.43188 USD of model API calls, of which 0.43175 USD was the web search that sourced its inputs.

Not covered by this study

  • Grid stability: frequency and voltage control, grid forming inverters and protection for hours with no diesel running
  • Land ownership, permits and the environmental review
  • Site layout, shading, soil and access for construction
  • Shipping to the island, local labour and construction time
  • Financing terms, tariffs, subsidies and who pays
  • Demand growth beyond the growth case, and new loads such as electric boats
  • Battery ageing beyond the replacement rule, and salt air corrosion
  • Diesel price risk beyond the sensitivity table
  • Diesel overhaul and engine replacement costs, which grow with engine hours
